SodaTide Ingredients

🍁

Chicory Root Inulin

Chicory root inulin is one of the principal ingredients identified in the published SodaTide formula. Inulin is a naturally occurring type of fermentable dietary fiber obtained from plants such as chicory. It is considered a prebiotic because certain microorganisms in the digestive tract can use it as a food source. According to currently published SodaTide label information, the formula contains 211 mg of chicory root inulin per capsule. Once consumed, inulin is not fully digested in the upper digestive tract, allowing part of it to reach the colon where fermentation can occur.
🍁

Potato Resistant Starch

Potato resistant starch is another highlighted component of SodaTide. Resistant starch differs from ordinary starch because some of it resists digestion in the small intestine. As a result, it can reach the large intestine, where microorganisms can ferment it. This characteristic makes resistant starch relevant to research involving gut bacteria, fermentation, and intestinal health. 
🍁

Probiotic Blend

The third major component is a probiotic blend. Current product information describes this as a 36 mg blend containing three probiotic strains, although online SodaTide pages do not all provide identical strain information.
🍁

Combines Prebiotics and Probiotics

SodaTide's formula is designed around the idea of combining prebiotic and probiotic components in the same product. Prebiotics provide fermentable material that can be used by microorganisms, while probiotics are consumed live microorganisms selected for potential health effects. Combining these categories is commonly referred to as a synbiotic approach. 
🍁

Uses a Non-Stimulant Approach

SodaTide is promoted as a non-stimulant supplement, with currently published product information stating that caffeine and stimulant ingredients are not part of the highlighted formula. This is relevant for people who prefer to avoid caffeine-based products or stimulant-heavy weight-management formulas. Instead of attempting to produce an immediate energy or thermogenic sensation, SodaTide focuses on prebiotic fiber, resistant starch, and probiotics.
